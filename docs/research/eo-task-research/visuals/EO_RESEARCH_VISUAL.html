01 / Scientific bet

Forecast change. Choose useful evidence.

Conditional · data gate unresolved

Which available radar or optical scene would improve a 20–30-day greenness forecast enough to justify processing it?

Select a stage

Each stage has a rule that keeps the replay temporally honest.
  1. 1. Past observationsDated radar/optical history available at the cutoff.
  2. 2. ForecastProbability and uncertainty for future greenness decline.
  3. 3. Choose S1, S2 or stopCandidate pixels remain hidden until selection.
  4. 4. Reveal and updateProcess the chosen archived scene; update the forecast.
  5. 5. Check future truthOnly now reveal future target observations for scoring.

The possible contribution

Predict task-specific observation value under missing sensors and geographic/time shift—not just future pixels or generic uncertainty.

Data starting point

GreenEarthNet for optical forecasting; EarthNet2023 Africa is a candidate for multisensor replay. Native scene chronology, future targets and rights are unverified.

What success would have to show

Better proper forecast scores per processing cost than cheap scene-selection rules, with honest calibration on held-out sites/dates.

Why it might fail

No usable radar/optical chronology; obscured target truth; future-weather leakage; or newest/clearest scenes work just as well.

Space scope: curated historical replays first. This is not a live global forecast, satellite tasking or land-management intervention.

Already exists: Contextformer, probabilistic NDVI forecasting, EO-WM and dynamic tasking. Novelty must come from a demonstrable observation-value method or evaluation capability.

02 / Scientific bet

Did the coast move—or just the waterline?

Conditional · matched surveys needed

When can two satellite views support a signed change in a surveyed mean-sea-level beach contour?

Select a stage

The output is a contestable physical claim, not just a change mask.
  1. 1. Paired views and surveysCompatible datum, fixed transects and close survey timing.
  2. 2. Check nuisance explanationsTides, waves, slope, proxies and shared registration errors.
  3. 3. Estimate physical change boundsCompare the interval against independent survey support.
  4. 4. Issue a direction—or abstainUnresolved direction does not mean unchanged.

A documented job, not imagined savings

8–10 hours of manual screening at difficult Barter Island in the USGS report; setup could take weeks. Software learning/development contributed. No savings from our method have been measured.

Data starting point

Published sandy-beach surveys. Narrabeen and San Diego Dryad metadata are CC0; imagery, tides/waves, matching and derivative rights still need clearance.

What success would have to show

More supported signed-change claims at the same false-claim rate than CoastSat/CoastSeg QC and conservative correlated physical-error bounds.

Why it might fail

Insufficient close-time survey matches; incompatible datums; or simple physical bounds are equally effective. Reef-lined Waikīkī is not a sandy-beach test.

Space scope: curated paired evidence or bounded numeric inputs → change interval / abstention → uncertainty explanations and reproducible export. No global erosion or attribution claim.

Already exists: CoastSat/CoastSeg, benchmark comparisons, tide-aware extraction and confidence displays. The proposed difference is the validity of the physical conclusion.

03 / Scientific bet

Find historical analogues—or say “no match”.

Conditional · novelty and truth weak

Can one dated burn example find verified cross-site patterns better than cheap spectral, shape and metadata search?

Select a stage

Similarity must survive independent relevance and genuine absence checks.
  1. 1. One dated event exampleBefore/after/later imagery and a defined visible pattern.
  2. 2. Search a fixed archiveSeparate query and gallery sites/events.
  3. 3. Verify the analogueIndependent relevance and footprint checks.
  4. 4. Matches—or no matchReturn dated evidence, not an unsupported explanation.

The possible contribution

A benchmark for rare, verified cross-site patterns with genuinely absent galleries. Generic archive search alone is not enough.

Data starting point

MTBS and Landsat, with separately cleared assets and independently judged relevance. Start with observable burn shapes—not shared cause.

What success would have to show

Better verified top-five results than metadata, spectral-change and shape filters, without accepting absent event patterns.

Why it might fail

Cheap search already works; relevance is subjective; or there are too few null cases. A four-query pilot cannot establish archive-wide reliability.

Space scope: search a small pinned historical archive and inspect dated source footprints. Not global live search, causal diagnosis or a calibrated probability from raw similarity.

Already exists: composed EO retrieval, OpenDPR and Earth Index. A public temporal-search demo also exists; its synthetic corpus is not real-world validation.

05 Flood-area evidence audit

Independent truth unresolved

A better map—or a better area estimate?

Need: a fixed probability-sampling budget and synchronized independent reference evidence.

Missing: expert SAR masks/model-derived labels are not automatically independent physical truth.

Do not infer: new scientific value from another flood-segmentation model alone.

Common lesson: the bottleneck is often the validity of the evidence—not model size. A polished map cannot repair missing ground truth.

Where our existing Clean-JEPA idea belongs

  1. RepresentationSensor-grounded featuresTest real S1/S2 availability, not channel hiding in fused annual embeddings.
  2. PredictionFuture state + uncertaintyA Gaussian latent marginal does not establish semantic alignment or calibration.
  3. Information actionChoose the next evidenceValue is task-specific score improvement, not generic confidence alone.
  4. Safe updatesCompatibility checksRetain Q2 as infrastructure. Reject an upgrade when task evidence is insufficient.

Earn the demo, one gate at a time

Recommended next action: metadata-only feasibility for forecast + observe. If chronology, future truth or rights fail, stop that formulation; coastal matched-survey feasibility is a separate alternative.

All ledgers are proposals. Feasible dates, source approvals and accepted splits are not established. Tiny pilots can reject a bad bet; they cannot certify an operational product.

What each pilot should be compared against

Forecast: persistence/seasonal/weather-history predictors; stop, random, newest, predicted-usability, always-S1/S2 and simple information-gain rules.

Coast: CoastSat/CoastSeg quality control, tide/slope/wave correction, correlated physical-error bounds and always-abstain.

Search: MTBS metadata, dNBR/NBR distributions, shape descriptors and frozen-feature search with equal quality screening.

Stop or pivot if: source/chronology/independent truth fails; gains disappear against cheap baselines; success relies on future information; or uncertainty is too wide to conclude. “Inconclusive” is not a win.

Evidence vocabulary

Documented
Supported by a cited source or metadata check—not necessarily reproduced here.
Proposed
A hypothesis, workflow, ledger or threshold to test.
Unresolved
A gate has not passed. It is not permission to train or publish.

Important limits

No user-demand validation, saved-hours measurement, benchmark result or adoption claim. A historical replay is not live inference. Field surveys do not automatically license imagery or ancillary products. No NASA-level impact prediction.
