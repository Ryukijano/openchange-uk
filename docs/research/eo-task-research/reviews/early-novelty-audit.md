# Decision: test compatibility first; conditionally pursue forest continuity

**Verdict: PIVOT, not a full-project GO.** The strongest eventual utility is keeping a previously validated forest-monitoring head and historical feature archive usable when the encoder changes or optical evidence disappears. The cheapest first falsifier is **same-year TESSERA version migration on an existing crop-map task**. This tests Q2 directly; it neither establishes Q3 nor makes crop mapping the final research contribution.

Candidate contribution: *Unlike generic embedding alignment and optical→SAR classification transfer, preserve a validated spatial head’s decisions against unchanged historical evidence across model/sensor transitions, with measured false-alert risk and lifecycle cost.* Without those measurements, this is mostly glue. This is separate from the already proposed forecast-residual detector versus RADD.

**Gates:** open = provisional yes for dense transition-safety evaluation, no for generic alignment; contribution = unclear until cost/risk results; feasible = yes for a small released-feature pilot after metadata/license checks, not yet for operational forest triage. My qualitative novelty/feasibility/impact/fit scores (1–5, judgments not measurements): compatibility pilot **2/5/3/5**, forest continuity **3/2/4/5**, coastal QA **2/3/4/2**, generic retrieval **1/3/2/2**.

## 1. Concrete workflow and matched baseline

An analyst has an optical-trained spatial forest head, its thresholds, and an old feature archive. New observations arrive from a newer encoder or from SAR during cloud. Uncorrected feature differences can generate apparent disturbances unrelated to environmental change. Map new spatial features into the old head/archive coordinate system; reject unsupported transitions; present dated evidence and uncertainty rather than silently replacing the validated system.

Later, select **which already archived sensor/date to read or which case to send for human annotation**. Charge bytes transferred, preprocessing/inference, annotation and review time. Revealing an already cached vector is much cheaper than reading its raw scene; never charge raw-processing savings after using that scene’s precomputed embedding for free. Sentinel acquisition itself is not a purchased/taskable action here.

**Matched baselines:** (a) retain old encoder/head; (b) naïvely mix new features with the old head/history; (c) Procrustes, ridge and a small nonlinear map fitted to identical unlabeled paired anchors; (d) re-encode history and retrain/revalidate the head; (e) retrain the head while retaining published embeddings, where applicable. Report label budgets, archive size, bytes and compute separately, with amortized break-even versus archive size and upgrade frequency.

For subsequent triage, use the **same features, actions, candidate alerts, labels, stopping constraints and cost schedule** for a chronological/quality-first rule and a greedy expected-error-reduction-per-cost policy. RADD supplies an operational disturbance/confirmation comparator, not a matched embedding-migration baseline: [official RADD description](https://www.wur.nl/en/research/products-services/radd-forest-disturbance-alert).

**Two distinct savings:** mapping new queries can avoid historical re-embedding; mapping new features can preserve an old head. Neither improves information already discarded by the old archive. A better query may improve retrieval, but it cannot retroactively turn old vectors into new-model vectors. Published TESSERA embeddings shift upstream inference cost to the publisher: claim local retraining/revalidation savings, not avoided global inference.

## 3. Novelty judgments and workflow choice

1. **Adaptive observation/analyst triage: UNCLEAR.** Generic active learning and cost-aware acquisition are closed. A controlled demonstration of reduced evidence-review cost while preserving transition safety remains provisionally open; no direct full-source match was found. A forecast residual is not automatically an action-value estimate or a world-action model.
2. **Missing-sensor/version head reuse: UNCLEAR overall; generic alignment CLOSED.** Zero/few-new-label dense-head migration, falsifiable safety checks and archive-preserving comparisons could support an efficiency/negative-result paper. Simply composing Procrustes with CROMA-like features would be glue.
3. **Same-scene retrieval/registration: CLOSED as a generic pitch.** Known coordinates already solve same-location lookup. [HOMO-Feature, ICCV 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Gao_HOMO-Feature_Cross-Arbitrary-Modal_Image_Matching_with_Homomorphism_of_Organized_Major_Orientation_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf) and [CRFT, CVPR 2026](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Liu_CRFT_Consistent-Recurrent_Feature_Flow_Transformer_for_Cross-Modal_Image_Registration_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf) further crowd registration. Geometric correspondence does not prove semantic head compatibility.

**Coastal QA:** strongest documented cost anchor. [USGS OFR 2025-1054](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ofr20251054/full) reports weeks of setup, then minutes–hours analysis; manual filtering and parameter adjustment remain burdens. But the [2025 suitability dataset](https://cmgds.marine.usgs.gov/catalog/pcmsc/DataReleases/CMGDS_DR_tool/DR_P1N4VI7H/segmentation_filter_metadata.html) was already used to train a segmentation-quality classifier. Its PNGs lose geospatial metadata; labels concern optical segmentation suitability, not persistent change. SAR cannot certify optical shoreline quality, tide correction or wave effects. Prefer coastal QA only for a demonstrated upgrade/domain-shift review problem beyond that existing classifier.

**Forest:** stronger Q2+Q3 fit, weaker immediate data feasibility. [MultiEarth](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.04738) supplies paired sensors and spatial deforestation masks, but only **11 truth slices in 2016–2021**; reject daily onset/lead-time claims and resolve label redistribution rights. [DISFOR](https://essd.copernicus.org/preprints/essd-2026-185/) has 3,822 temporally segmented European points, interpreter confidence and clusters, but releases S2 data—not ready-made paired SAR or dense polygons—and excludes low-confidence interpretations. It cannot validate RADD’s tropical operating regime by itself.

## 4. Limits specific to Clean-JEPA

Keep the shared ViT, separate S1/S2 patch stems, modality embeddings, no main-arm teacher/predictor, 2-global/6-local invariance and availability-stratified SIGReg. FP32 loss and BF16 GH200 DDP are implementation choices, not novelty.

The later frozen future-latent predictor should receive elapsed time and context. It could help estimate evidence utility, but needs action-conditioned uncertainty/error validation before driving a reveal policy. Archive-only actions can support an observation-policy benchmark, not claims about controlling Earth dynamics.

Gaussian marginals do not identify semantic correspondence: label permutations and nonlinear distribution-preserving transforms remain possible. Pair anchors and held-out task checks are necessary. Apply maps at the **actual spatial features consumed by the head**; alignment of a pooled/projector vector does not certify dense boundaries. Orthogonal maps cannot repair changed geometry, token layout or missing information. SAR substitution has irreducible error where optical-only evidence distinguishes otherwise identical SAR observations. Strong crop/season invariance can also erase localized disturbance signals.

Cloud/missingness is not random; test natural cloud regimes as well as controlled masks. Split by geography, acquisition/event cluster and future time; keep alignment anchors separate from head labels and evaluation. Annual embeddings contain future observations relative to within-year alerts. Near-coincident optical/SAR pairs can still bracket real change. Keep **Q1 label-free checkpoint selection separate**: downstream safety labels cannot repeatedly tune its proxy.

## 5. Smallest falsifier and three kill criteria

**First experiment, proposed—not run:** one labeled crop-map region/year; matched TESSERA `1.0-vultr` and `1.1-dclimate` embeddings. [Current raw README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ucam-eo/geotessera/main/README.md) confirms those releases; [official CLI](https://geotessera.readthedocs.io/en/stable/cli_reference.html) forbids interchanging versions/variants. Verify checkpoint provenance, preprocessing, grid/year overlap and dataset licenses first: variants may represent different inference runs, not an encoder upgrade.

Train one old spatial head; freeze it. Fit Procrustes/ridge from 50/100/500 spatially separated unlabeled anchor pairs; compare old-head migration with naïve mixing and new-head retraining at matched label budgets. Measure macro-F1/IoU, boundary errors, truth-backed wrong transitions, same-year map churn, calibration and full local cost. Same-year churn is not a validated forest alert. Spatial-block bootstrap; no random neighboring-pixel split.

Audit anchor rank/coverage: fewer than 128 independent anchors cannot identify a full 128-dimensional orthogonal map. Include a no-migration control; report unsupported directions rather than hiding them with a favorable mean cosine score.

Then test Q3 on raw paired PASTIS-R or legally usable forest data—not by hiding channels of fused annual embeddings. Only after those gates add archived-observation policy and timed expert review. With released embeddings the first pilot is CPU-sized and plausibly under a week; new Clean-JEPA pretraining is a separate, uncosted gate, not required for this falsifier.

**Pre-register these kill criteria:**
1. Stop Q2 if migration cannot stay within **2 percentage points** of matched retraining on held-out macro-F1/IoU, or fails to reduce naïve transition errors, including boundaries.
2. Stop the savings claim if mapping, anchor preparation and revalidation cost eliminates local savings; without timed users, report review-count proxies only.
3. Stop forest/policy expansion if rights or chronology remain unresolved, or if matched greedy/rule baselines retain equal safety at equal/lower cost. Dense daily-alert claims are already unsupported.

**Settled recommendation:** TESSERA Q2 falsifier first; forest continuity only conditionally; coastal QA a control, retrieval/registration not a lead. Architecture-specific superiority and expert-time savings remain untested.

A useful eventual demo would show an unchanged-location before/after-version comparison, the preserved head, real dated SAR/optical evidence, cost counters and explicit abstention. Milestones: metadata/rank smoke check → one-block pilot → held-out geography/seeds and transition ablations → real expert timing. No code, imagery/embedding downloads or HPC runs were performed in this audit.
