# Disaster transfer: independently audited flood area, not another flood-map demo

**13 October 2026 · topic_id: disaster_transfer · Verdict: PIVOT.** One candidate survives as a question, not yet as an approved model project. Generic flood segmentation, multimodal fusion, transfer testing and confidence/abstention are already occupied. The narrower opportunity is an evidence-budget benchmark and usable **area-audit instrument**. Independent reference support and useful precision remain unproven.

## 1. Scientific question and contribution

**In a genuinely unseen flood event, can a transferred pre/post Sentinel-1 map make a small independent probability sample substantially more informative about newly inundated open-water area than sampling alone or a cheap change detector?** The regime is **event/geographical transfer**, not missing-sensor robustness or flood forecasting. Restrict the estimand to a declared, georeferenced AOI and observation date; do not claim total inundation beneath buildings or vegetation that the reference cannot observe.

Hypothesis: at the same evidence budget, frozen transferred maps reduce the uncertainty of a design-based, map-assisted area estimate across held-out events, without counting permanent water as new flooding. Reference labels audit the map; they do not fine-tune it. Model-derived map totals alone are not scientific estimates.

The potentially publishable delta is an **event-blocked, reference-audited comparison of representation quality as survey information**, including negative results: when impressive segmentation scores fail to improve an independently supported area estimate. This is not a new estimator—survey statistics already supplies that machinery—and not a new confidence layer. Researchers might adopt a compact protocol because it exposes event leakage, reference circularity and evidence requirements while testing existing checkpoints without another large training campaign. Merely packaging established statistics in Gradio would not justify a paper.

## 2. Real job and burden

The user is an EO researcher or post-event flood analyst checking a mapped area before using it in an impact study. Their job is to distinguish newly flooded land from standing water, reconcile acquisition times and assess how much a transferred mask can be trusted quantitatively.

[Kuro Siwo, main text and supplement](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.12056v3) documents five SAR experts, cross-checking by another annotator, senior supervision and discrepancy meetings for 43 events; it explicitly calls acquisition and photointerpretation costly. This is **qualitative burden**, not measured hours or money. [CEMS's 2025 QA announcement, 27 July 2026](https://european-flood.emergency.copernicus.eu/news/publication-of-the-global-flood-monitoring-annual-product-and-service-quality-assessment-report-2025) reports 12 validation cases: reference-water CSI falls to 31.3%, 53.1% and 38.9% in three cases. That establishes a real validation job, not adoption of this proposal.

Success could matter more than encoder-continuity reviewing because it addresses the correctness of a consequential scientific quantity on a new event, rather than preserving an existing head after an upgrade. No evidence here demonstrates saved analyst time, emergency benefit, demand for this Space or NASA-like adoption.

## 3. Five closest primary works

1. **Olofsson et al., Remote Sensing of Environment, 2014**, [journal](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0034425714000704), [author manuscript](https://nottingham-repository.worktribe.com/preview/728232/Olofsson_good%20practices.pdf). Probability sampling, better reference evidence and uncertainty-aware area estimation are established; our possible delta is their stringent evaluation under modern flood-event transfer, not inventing area correction.
2. **Bountos et al., Kuro Siwo / BlackBench, NeurIPS 2024 Datasets and Benchmarks**, [primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.12056v3), [proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/43612b0662cb6a4986edf859fd6ebafe-Abstract.html). Already holds out ten events and evaluates strong conventional models; delta is sparse independent area evidence, not another event split. Its May 2023 Italy example compares SAR on 22 May with optical on 23 May visually, not a synchronized quantitative truth set.
3. **Astruc et al., AnySat, CVPR 2025**, [accepted proceedings](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Astruc_AnySat_One_Earth_Observation_Model_for_Many_Resolutions_Scales_and_CVPR_2025_paper.html), [methods/evaluation](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.14123v2). Already demonstrates strong linear probing on external flood data; delta is useful survey information on strictly unseen events, not JEPA or parameter efficiency.
4. **Li, Matgen and Chini, IEEE TGRS 64, article 4202520, 2026**, [authors' institutional record](https://researchportal.list.lu/publications/detail/quantifying-and-communicating-uncertainty-in-sar-based-flood-mapping-via-density-aware-neural-networks-and-conformal-risk-control). Already combines density-aware OOD abstention with conformal prediction sets; delta must be independently audited area estimation, not confidence or abstention. Only the institutional abstract was accessible here; calibration details remain unchecked.
5. **Chiriaco et al., GEOID-Flood, 3 August 2026**, [full primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02315v1), [repository](https://github.com/links-ads/geoid-flood). Authors report ECCV 2026 TerraBytes workshop acceptance, not main-conference acceptance. Already tests 2026 held-out events, harmonizes RTC preprocessing and compares foundation models; delta is evidence-budget utility and independent reference support, not transfer alone.

Decisive sections read: Kuro data/BlackBench/annotation supplement; AnySat §§4.1–4.2; GEOID §§3–5 and permanent-water appendix. Six bounded search rounds included recent CVPR, ICLR/OpenReview, NeurIPS and remote-sensing work. ICLR search hits included submissions, not verified acceptances. This is not an exhaustive novelty claim.

## 4. Data, weights and release traps

**Kuro Siwo:** 43 events, 2015–2022, six continents/three climate zones; 10 m, 224×224 chips, two pre-event and one post-event Sentinel-1 acquisitions, VV/VH and elevation; labels distinguish dry/permanent/flood water. [Official HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/orion-ai-lab/Kuro-Siwo-Webdataset/raw/main/README.md) offers `labelled_GRD`, `train_GRD/*.tar`, `test_GRD/*.tar`, plus `info.json` and validity masks. Original supplement §9 declares MIT for data/BlackBench; current author HF card declares CC-BY-4.0; [author polygon repository](https://github.com/Orion-AI-Lab/KuroSiwo-annotations) declares MIT. Pin the exact release and carry provenance; do not silently merge licence declarations. Sentinel imagery is upstream Copernicus data. Expert SAR interpretation is independent of our predictions, **not independent sensor/field truth**.

**GEOID-Flood:** January 2016–March 2026, 219 events/65 countries, 14,282 1024² tiles at 10 m. [Official card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/links-ads/geoid-flood/raw/main/README.md) exposes UTM/geometries/delineation times in `tile_catalog.parquet`; held-out EMSR857–871 are February–March 2026, 1,429 tiles. Access can select held-out S1-RTC/labels (~20 GB), but is shard-level, not a tiny tile download. Compilation/labels CC-BY-4.0, code MIT; Sentinel/CEMS notices remain required and DEM has a separate WorldDEM-30 licence.

Two decisive cautions: its permanent-water labels are decoded from annual embeddings, not independent truth; its card explicitly lists **EMSR712-10 as train and EMSR712-3 as test**, despite event-level wording. This documents same-activation split membership, not a quantified leakage effect. Do not inherit the split uncritically.

**Weights:** [AnySat](https://huggingface.co/g-astruc/AnySat), MIT, pretrained on five GeoPlex datasets including all unlabeled PASTIS-HD; spatial overlap with proposed flood sites remains unaudited. [TerraMind-base-Flood](https://huggingface.co/ibm-esa-geospatial/TerraMind-base-Flood/raw/main/README.md), Apache-2.0, now uses ImpactMesh-Flood, not the earlier FloodsNet configuration. Its [dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ibm-esa-geospatial/ImpactMesh-Flood/raw/main/README.md) identifies 206 events and a 562-sample event-exclusive subset: exact event IDs must be cross-checked. [Prithvi flood weights](https://huggingface.co/ibm-nasa-geospatial/Prithvi-100M-sen1floods11/raw/main/README.md) are Apache-2.0 but optical, binary water/flood and trained on Sen1Floods11; they are not a drop-in SAR or transient-water baseline. Sen1Floods11 redistribution conflict remains a blocker. No robust deployable checkpoint is established.

## 5. Falsifier: at most 480 scored observations

Before inspecting outcomes, freeze **six event-AOIs: two development, four sealed test**, with distinct basins/geographies; source fitting uses only earlier events. Test events must be absent from every supervised training set. Record acquisition IDs, dates, geometries, orbit, preprocessing and hashes; reject touching/overlapping source areas. Exact sites are not yet verified.

Use 20 probability-sampled reference locations per event. Conservatively count each site separately at **two dates × two sensors** (SAR input plus independent time-matched optical reference): **6×20×2×2 = 480**. Shared pixels, repeated dates and second sensors are not extra independent samples; additional observations exceed the budget. No extra test labels for tuning. Compare frozen threshold/change masks, small U-Net, available GFM/probe, and sampling without a model. Keep normalization and observable AOI identical; GFM feasibility is conditional on weights/input support.

Primary pilot outcomes: design-weighted area-estimate interval width and map-assisted residual variance versus sampling alone, plus sampled commission/omission errors. Use known inclusion probabilities, finite-population/design-based uncertainty and event-wise reporting—not pixel-bootstrap or pooled pseudo-replication. Unresolved reference classes contribute worst-case bounds; do not discard inconvenient sites. Existing dense polygon labels may support resampling simulations, explicitly **surrogate-reference agreement**, not physical coverage guarantees. Four test events cannot establish universal 95% coverage.

Kill if time-matched independent evidence or permissions cannot be established; if intervals stay too wide for a preregistered scientific use; or if cheap maps/sampling match or dominate transferred representations. A smaller interval without trustworthy reference labels is failure, not success. No thresholds above are achieved results.

## 6. Public HF instrument

**AOI/date + pinned map + sampled evidence → audited area estimate, unresolved-area bounds and downloadable provenance/sampling ledger.** Curated, rights-cleared replays work without login, with revealed reference labels for checking calculations. New users select allowlisted catalogue IDs and supply bounded CSV evidence rows at requested coordinates; self-selected points are marked non-probability evidence and cannot receive design-based claims.

CPU executes sampling/statistics. Optional approved checkpoint inference is capped at four 256² tiles/job on ZeroGPU, with proposed 60-second timeout—not measured latency. Reject archives, pickle, executable inputs, arbitrary URLs and unbounded rasters. Validate CRS, timestamps, band units and alignment. Process evidence ephemerally, remove identifiers, disclose public location sensitivity, and export licences/model/data revisions. Abstain on unsupported dates, missing provenance or inadequate truth. This is retrospective analysis, **not live emergency guidance**. It does something beyond serving a mask: tells a user which quantity their evidence supports and lets them reproduce the estimate.

## 7. Subjective scores and final decision

**Potential impact 4/5:** meaningful scientific area accounting; no proven benefit/adoption. **Scientific openness 2/5:** estimators and transfer are established; benchmark novelty uncertain. **Feasibility 2/5:** imagery exists, independent reference timing/rights and precision do not yet pass. **Demo usefulness 4/5:** a reproducible audit is usable if inputs are valid. **WAM fit 2/5:** later observation selection could be an information action; this neither predicts dynamics nor establishes causal restoration effects.

**PIVOT, not CONDITIONAL-GO for a new flood model.** Advance only the small reference/protocol falsifier if independent evidence can be verified. Otherwise NO-GO. The limiting unknown is trustworthy, time-matched reference evidence—not a fashionable encoder or loss. Research only: no data/weights downloaded, no training, repository changes or deployment performed.
