# Coastal foundation-model data: feasibility and rights

Metadata/primary-source audit, 13 October 2026. No imagery or weights downloaded; no training, cluster access, publication or author contact. Counts below are source statements unless marked **audit** or **proposal**. Licensing notes are a provenance screen, not legal advice.

## Verdict

There is enough openly usable **Landsat/Sentinel coastal imagery** to test continued pretraining of an existing EO encoder. There is not yet a verified, diverse collection of independent surveyed beaches sufficient to call a four-site error head a coastal foundation model. Start with the survey-calibrated reliability gap, then test whether coastal adaptation transfers to segmentation, coastal classification and measured change. JEPA is optional; an LLM/RLHF is unnecessary.

The main risk is not image quantity: it is **few independent beaches, mismatched shoreline definitions, overlapping training data, and third-party imagery rights**.

## What counts as independent evidence?

- Verified monitored geography currently totals **4 SDS beaches + Kahaloa + 2 additional San Diego study areas = 7 study areas**, not seven independent coastal regimes. Five Spanish CoastSnap sites add photographic labels, not five new survey-truth sites. USGS lidar may add many coastal cells; the number is **not established** by this metadata audit.
- Count and report **regions, littoral cells, beaches, survey campaigns, acquisition dates, sensors**, then transect rows. Bootstrap by beach/cell and date/campaign, not independent 10-m points; publish site-wise scores and worst-site results.
- No shoreline is universal: instantaneous waterline, foam/wet–dry boundary, beach toe/LWM, and MSL/MHW/MHHW elevation contours are distinct targets. Train proxy-specific heads or explicitly map through measured slope/tide with uncertainty. Dune/marsh labels do not identify a beach waterline automatically.
- **Selection bias:** submitted SDS shorelines may already exclude failed/discarded images. Audit each method's acquisition denominator, QC/rejection records and missingness before training usability or abstention. Errors among accepted submissions support only conditional reliability; absence is not automatically a QC-failure label. A reject detector needs actual rejected cases with auditable labels, not just accepted shoreline/survey pairs.
- Waikīkī's quoted DTM CI around a mean is **not the per-pixel elevation error**, and total-station instrument precision is **not beach-toe identification error**. Carry survey uncertainty separately from model error.

## Proposed split contract (not executed)

1. **Four-site reliability pilot:** rotate four outer folds: 2 train beaches, 1 calibration beach, 1 untouched test beach. Tune only within the two training beaches using contiguous date blocks. Fit interval/abstention thresholds only on calibration. Report every fold; no distribution-free new-site guarantee from four sites. Reserve Kahaloa as an additional untouched reef-fringed test after target harmonisation.
2. **Corpus geography:** keep **all calibration/test coasts out of continued pretraining**, across every date and sensor. Reserve whole littoral cells and whole neighbouring coastal stretches; start with a **50-km exclusion buffer**, enlarge if patch footprints, scene context, regional duplication or cell boundaries require it. This distance is a conservative proposal, not a validated decorrelation length. Reject any model input footprint intersecting a reserved area. Include overlapping CoastTrain/Doodleverse/QC data in this check.
3. **Date split on training geography:** example train 2017–2020, tune 2021, calibration 2022, test 2023–2024 for later temporal-generalisation tests. Use **90-day embargoes** around cutoffs and keep same-scene/tile, survey campaign, storm episode and temporal-sequence members together. Expand if measured autocorrelation lasts longer; do not let time-series interpolation cross folds. Kahaloa instead uses its entire 2018–2021 record as geography-held-out evidence, not the later-date split.
4. **Pretrained-backbone caveat:** an existing global encoder may have seen reserved coasts. Audit its training manifest; if unknown, state “no held-out coast in our continued pretraining,” not “never seen geography.” A strict never-seen test needs verifiable upstream exclusions. Compare the original encoder and adapted encoder under the same disclosed upstream conditions.
5. Tide/waves are **acquisition-time physical covariates**, not ecological causal labels. Interpolate independently within split, record timezone, tide datum, wave location/model provenance and missingness. Forecasting uses only information available at the forecast origin; future observed tide/wave records cannot silently become inputs.

## Tasks needed before a coastal foundation-model claim

One shared encoder must transfer with small task-specific heads/adapters and fixed label budgets, across unseen regions. Do not rebrand one four-site error regressor.

| Task / target | Independent evidence | Required metrics and baselines |
|---|---|---|
| Coastal water/whitewater/sediment segmentation and proxy-specific shoreline localisation | Disjoint source footprints/regions; masks for semantics, surveys for physical position | Per-class IoU/macro IoU; boundary F-score with fixed metre tolerance; signed bias, MAE/RMSE and 95th-percentile transect displacement. CoastSat index/classifier and CoastSeg/Doodleverse segmentation, plus original EO encoder. |
| Per-observation error distribution and safe abstention | Matched geodetic survey targets, held-out beaches, independent calibration beach | CRPS or NLL; 50/80/95% interval coverage **and width**; probability-of-error-threshold Brier score; risk–coverage curve/AURC and coverage at a fixed positional risk. CoastSeg learned QC, simple metadata head, physical tide/slope/runup/registration error budget (including existing RSMA framework) and frozen encoder. |
| Coastal attribute classification (distinct transfer task) | Expert CoastBench labels split by region/cell; audit imagery assets first | Macro F1, balanced accuracy, per-class confusion, Brier/ECE, regional worst-case and 1/5/10/100%-label-budget curves. Published multi-task CNN, original EO encoder, random-init task model. Predicted global typology cannot be its own truth. |
| Measured shoreline/morphology change between dates (not causal ecological change) | Two independently surveyed datum-aligned dates; USGS lidar/Kahaloa, non-overlapping campaign groups | Change MAE/RMSE in metres, signed bias, interval coverage/width, precision/recall for change beyond the survey-derived noise floor; false alarm rate on stable pairs. Persistence/no-change, subtraction of CoastSat/CoastSeg positions and non-neural uncertainty propagation. |

Require gains on at least **three genuinely distinct tasks**, label-efficiency and unseen-region tests, with ablations of coastal pretraining versus the exact original encoder. Segmentation, a line extracted from that mask, and its IoU are not three independent tasks. “Better” must name metric, baseline, target/proxy and regime; no global superiority claim from sandy open-ocean sites.

## Bounded corpus pilot and scaling estimate — proposals, not downloads

- Build a licence/provenance **manifest first**: product/scene ID, asset URL, source licence, attribution, band order, radiometry/product level, resolution, acquisition UTC, CRS/footprint, cloud/valid fraction, coastline/cell ID, split, checksum when eventually fetched. Use licensed Sentinel/Landsat pixels, not scraped web basemaps. Reacquire open multispectral bands where a RGB-only label can be aligned; verify georegistration rather than assuming it.
- Pilot: **100 training-coast blocks × up to 20 usable acquisitions = at most 2,000 chips**; balance coast type, continent, tide range and cloud conditions. Include hard/cloud/foam cases, not only “pretty clear” images. This is a target, not measured archive yield or 100 surveyed beaches. First cheap check: catalogue-only valid-date counts and footprints after exclusions.
- Storage arithmetic: 256×256 pixels × 6 bands × uint16 = **786,432 bytes/chip** before compression. Thus 2,000 chips ≈ **1.57 GB**, 200,000 ≈ **157 GB**, 1 million ≈ **786 GB** of pixel arrays; masks, QA, metadata, alternate sensors and preprocessing copies are additional. These figures are crop-size calculations, **not full-scene download estimates**. Three-frame sequences cost triple if represented without frame reuse.
- At 16-pixel patches this is 256 spatial tokens/frame, 768 tokens/three frames. Actual compute depends on whether time is stacked, independently encoded or attended jointly. Joint full attention is not linearly scalable with sequence length.
- Budget via measurements: steps = epochs × ceil(chips / effective batch). At 10 epochs and batch 64: **320 / 31,250 / 156,250 updates** for 2k / 200k / 1m chips, assuming one chip per example. Convert using measured seconds/update, loader wait and peak memory on the selected hardware; do not invent exact GPU hours. Profile a fixed sample of batches before authorising expansion.
- Gate: first frozen existing encoder + metadata head. Only continue-pretrain if a measured held-out gap remains and the 2k pilot improves downstream transfer against no-adaptation under equal label/compute budgets. Scale geographic diversity before repeated neighbouring dates. Random-init foundation pretraining is a separate, expensive experiment gated by evidence that licensed diverse data and adaptation have plateaued; random-init small task models remain valid baselines.
