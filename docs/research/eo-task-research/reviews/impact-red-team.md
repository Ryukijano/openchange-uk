# Red-team decision: a claim about change, not another change map

**13 October 2026 · Read-only review of five independent dossiers.** This ranks research *questions*, not deployed systems or measured gains. Eight primary-source checks below are my own; other details explicitly marked inherited were not independently reverified. No data, imagery or weights were downloaded, and no practitioner was contacted. The search is bounded, not a proof of universal novelty. The objective is a publishable scientific capability with a usable public Hugging Face Space; the Space alone is not the contribution.

## Pareto shortlist

| Rank | Sharply framed question | Subjective potential impact / openness / feasibility / Space utility / WAM fit (1–5) | Why retained, and immediate gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Coastal physical-change identifiability** | When can two Landsat/Sentinel-2 views support a *signed change in a surveyed local-MSL sandy-beach contour*, after allowing tide, waves, proxy choice, survey timing and correlated registration error? | **4 / 3 / 3 / 4 / 2** | A false physical conclusion is scientifically consequential; existing survey truth and an analyst QC burden are documented. Close-time scene/survey matches and improvement over cheap physical uncertainty bounds remain unproved. |
| **2. Forecast-then-observe rangeland decline** | Does processing one already-acquired, concealed S1 or S2 observation improve a calibrated 20–30-day greenness-decline forecast more per unit processing cost than simple sensor-choice rules? | **4 / 3 / 2 / 3 / 4** | Best route toward a georeferenced future state plus an *information* action, but African paired-scene chronology, target truth and archive rights have not been established. It is not orbital tasking or causal restoration. |
| **3. Historical burn analogue search** | Does a before/after/later Landsat exemplar find cross-site, independently adjudicated burn-pattern analogues at five inspected results per query better than quality-gated metadata, dNBR and shape search? | **3 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 1** | Most accessible search-first Space if a curated, lawful index exists. Scientific novelty and demand are weak until rare useful phenotypes and independent absent-gallery judgments are demonstrated. |

These are **not** totals to maximize: coastal offers physical truth, forecast/observe offers stronger dynamics-and-observation research but weaker data support, and search offers an immediately intelligible user action at lower novelty. **Highest potential scientific impact I would fund now:** coastal claim validity. **Lowest-risk usable demonstration:** its CPU physical-interval/abstention replay, *without* pretending a learned selector exists; among alternate domains, bounded burn search is the friendliest public interface. **Strongest eventual WAM path:** forecast/observe, conditional on actual timestamped paired sensor evidence. These distinctions do not make the current Clean-JEPA checkpoint a world model.

## Primary recommendation: conditional coastal pilot

**Hypothesis.** At a fixed false signed-change rate on genuinely unseen sandy coasts, a selector that represents competing shoreline proxies and *shared*, scene-level registration/water-level errors will retain more supportable change decisions than conservative physical error propagation alone. Predicting a waterline is not identifying a moved MSL contour. Neither movement nor a retreat sign establishes sediment loss, long-term erosion, storm attribution or a restoration effect. Cite [CoastSeg (JOSS 2024)](https://joss.theoj.org/papers/10.21105/joss.06683), Vos and Mikkelsen as direct workflow/physics incumbents; [NatShore (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jag.2026.105277) also claims tidal-aware extraction, so confidence maps do not establish novelty. If the physical baseline ties, do **not** add a learned model for aesthetics.

**Rights/data gate.** Obtain source-specific permissions and provenance for four published sandy sites: Narrabeen profiles via the Dryad record above; Truc Vert DEMs via the [dataset repository](https://osf.io/jftw8/) and descriptor; Torrey Pines via [San Diego Dryad](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.n5qb383); Duck via the SDS_Benchmark source pointers. Confirm exact local-MSL transformation, survey dates, source licences and scene IDs *before* packaging derivatives. Landsat USGS policy supports USGS-origin imagery; [Copernicus Sentinel terms](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/terms-and-conditions) are separate from a benchmark code licence. FES tides and ERA5/wave derivative redistribution must also be checked. No model weight or site-independent checkpoint is assumed; pretraining overlap is inapplicable to the physical-only baseline, and must be disclosed if a learned selector is later used.

**First falsifier, at most 480 scored records.** Freeze one metadata-derived manifest before inspecting errors: four sites × four fixed transects × twenty acquisition/date/sensor endpoints = **320 endpoint records**, plus ten non-overlapping paired changes per transect/site = **160 pair records**, **480 total**. Target ten Landsat-8 and ten Sentinel-2 acquisitions/site, with same-mission pairs and surveys within **48 hours** of each endpoint, or stop rather than silently widen to ten days. Four transects on one scene share the same scene error; pairs reuse endpoints, so there are only **80 acquisition clusters** at most, not 480 independent images. No extra sensors/dates or selected positive episodes hidden outside the count. The exact feasible dates are **not established**. Development Narrabeen, calibration Torrey Pines, and sealed geographically separated Duck + Truc Vert are a proposed partition subject to date/rights feasibility; freeze contiguous training/calibration time blocks and exclude footprint overlap. All four sites were previously benchmarked: this is *protocol development*, not a blind independent-new-coast performance claim. Waikīkī may probe explicitly OOD reefs but cannot rescue the sandy primary test or evade the 500-unit budget.

Compare CoastSat/CoastSeg-style QC, tide+slope and optional wave correction, robust no-change/trend, always-abstain, and Monte Carlo physical bounds carrying **correlated** registration, datum, survey-timing and water-level errors. Report paired-change interval coverage/width, false sign among issued claims, retained coverage and risk–coverage by **site/sensor**; bootstrap scene/date clusters and show site-level outcomes, not pixel pseudo-replication. Set prospective gates: if nominal 90% test intervals cover <85%, false signs >10% among issued claims, or retained coverage <25%, the claim-capable instrument fails. If selector coverage does not exceed conservative bounds at matched false-sign rate, kill the learned novelty. Small-site intervals may be too wide to conclude: “inconclusive” is not a win. If sufficient 48-hour matched scenes, compatible datum, upstream rights or survey support fail, **no-go for this pilot**; a reproducible resolution-limit study is worth publishing only if it adds a new, independently checkable finding beyond SDS_Benchmark.

**Actual public Space action.** No-login curated *historical replays* and a ≤1 MB numeric CSV of ≤100 endpoints with dates, CRS, sensor, local datum, physical nuisance bounds and provenance → bounded CPU uncertainty propagation → signed MSL-change interval **or abstention**, dominant unresolved explanations, survey comparison where legitimately available, and downloadable claim/scene/parameter manifest. Reject URLs, arbitrary imagery, archives and executables; do not store precise private positions. Unsupported reef, inlet, structures, missing datum or unbounded intervening storm means abstain. New user rows are unverified claims, not secretly ground truth; no live coast-monitoring or cost-saving promise. The scientific value is contestable physical evidence, not a dashboard.

## Fallback and longer-horizon bet

**Fallback if coast fails:** MTBS/Landsat-only burn analogue search, conditional on lawful selected fire/scene assets and independent relevance/null annotation. Freeze the inherited **460-unit** proposal (64 gallery windows × three dated Landsat observations = 192; four query windows × three = 12; 4 × 64 judged pair decisions = 256); geographic/time splits must be fixed before judging. Compare MTBS metadata, matched-quality dNBR/shape, frozen RGB differences and temporal baselines at top five, including absent galleries. **Kill** if either positive query gains no verified top-five hit over cheap search, or any absent gallery is accepted; even perfect rejection on only two null queries cannot validate calibrated abstention. The no-login Space can search a small pinned archive and export dated scene IDs/footprints, *not* claim global live search. If cheap search wins, publish a benchmark only if its independently labelled failure analysis is reusable; otherwise no-go. It is a fallback for usability, not a stronger science claim than coast.

**Forecast/observe remains a research watch-list, not the fallback launch.** Before any model or Space, verify the exact Africa archive's S1/S2 scene timestamps and paired availability, rights, raw future optical target support, known-at-cutoff weather, and training-site overlap. Only then freeze 12 separate sites × two cutoffs × four availability strata × (two candidate sensor/date scenes + two future optical verification dates) = **384 scored slots**, explicitly counting repeated sites/dates/sensors. Compare stop, newest, random, always-S1/S2, uncertainty×usability and cheap weather/history forecasts using paired proper-score improvement and site-blocked calibration. Hiding a fused optical channel is **not** radar; revealing a scene is **not** collecting a new one. Kill if physical truth is sparse, rights fail or any apparent gain needs retrospective clouds/weather. This is the strongest WAM-*adjacent* trajectory, not evidence of a deployable world-action model.

**Rejected/narrowed:** restoration well-verified rewetting has real potential, but its inherited Dryad plot coordinates/intervention dates and separate NatureScot raw wells were not independently joined/licensed here; do not imply causal treatment effects from rainfall-adjusted satellite wetness, nor use intact bog as an untreated felled-forest control. Flood area auditing uses an established survey estimator and faces a missing independent synchronized reference; GEOID and Kuro do not resolve that by labelling themselves truth. These are **gates**, not negative experimental results. None of the five dossiers ran a performance test; none demonstrates HF adoption or saving analyst hours. A physical conclusion and reproducible refusal could be more consequential than maintaining task heads through an encoder upgrade, but actual utility must be demonstrated by blinded site transfer and practitioner review—not inferred from a polished interface.
