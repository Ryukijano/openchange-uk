# Can satellite monitoring distinguish peatland rewetting from a wet year?

**Topic:** `restoration_effect` · **Verdict:** **CONDITIONAL-GO for an independent, field-anchored falsifier; NO-GO for a causal-effects Space or a new map app today.** This is an audit of a possible programme, not a claim that a checkpoint, dataset join, or product works.

## Question, contribution, and boundary

In Scottish blanket bog restored after forestry or drainage, can satellite monitoring distinguish *field-verified water-table recovery* from rainfall/phenology **at unseen sites**, and abstain otherwise? Predeclare water-table depth relative to peat surface as the endpoint. Compare wells with meteorology and unrestored *drained* references; intact bog is a recovery target, **not** the untreated counterfactual to felled forest. A publishable contribution would be a versioned cross-site benchmark with selective prediction and documented failure modes, not another wetness map. Optical greenness is not peat condition, species recovery, carbon flux or biodiversity. This could matter more than the model-upgrade/continuity reviewer: managers need to decide whether a putative change merits field verification, not just preserve a task head through an encoder upgrade. That is **potential**, not proven adoption or savings.

## The job and its documented burden

[NatureScot Peatland ACTION](https://www.nature.scot/climate-change/nature-based-solutions/nature-based-solutions-practice/peatland-action/peatland-action-data-research-and-monitoring/peatland-action) lists water-table monitoring at **35 restoration sites since 2014**, rainfall gauges at **25** (some outages), and 2014–15 vegetation surveys (**1,002 quadrats/16 sites**) repeated in 2021 (**873/13 sites**) across restored, unrestored, and unmodified areas. Raw well/rain CSVs are **on request**. Sampling volume is documented; staff hours/money are **not**. A hydrologist could inspect an adjusted claim and select field revisits. No practitioner was contacted. A [2026 GEE practitioner app](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rsase.2025.101836) already provides before/after maps.

## Five closest works: what remains, if anything

1. [Isoaho et al., *Remote Sensing of Environment*, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2024.114144): Six restored and five control Finnish mires, 2015–23, Sentinel-1/2/Landsat random-forest water-table estimates and bootstrap contrasts; **delta:** independent Scottish well transfer and calibrated abstention. I read methods/evaluation. Their site-exclusion validation narrows novelty; field data are “available on request.” Their overall R²=.71/RMSE=6.01 cm is **their** result, not reproduced here.
2. [Hughes et al., *Journal of Applied Ecology*, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2664.70101), [author's methods chapter](https://pureadmin.uhi.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/65410727/HUGHES_R_PhD.pdf) and [Dryad data](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.qjq2bvqt2): Nine randomized plot groups at Forsinard compare four felling/blocking combinations with forest/bog references; **delta:** weather-controlled imagery prediction and cross-region limits, not rediscovery of treatment results. I read chapter 6 design/analysis. Baseline 2012–13; plot interventions 2014–19; monitoring rounds 2013/14/17/21. **2017 is not post-intervention at every plot.**
3. [Räsänen et al., *Restoration Ecology*, online 2024/volume 2025](https://doi.org/10.1111/rec.14338): Finnish 150-site network; species/PFT associations with red/NIR, STR, SAVI, NDMI are weak; **delta:** test well-based recovery, not biodiversity from spectra. I read data/model/evaluation sections. Restoration 2007–14, inventories at years 0/2/5/10; raw-network redistribution unverified.
4. [Isoaho et al., *Remote Sensing Applications: Society and Environment*, accepted 2025/volume 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rsase.2025.101836): [GEE app](https://aleksiisoaho.users.earthengine.app/view/optical-satellite-before-after-visualisation), 24 Finnish sites restored 2018–23 and 2014–24 optical imagery; **delta:** independent wells, weather controls, refusal/triage. I read methods/limits: tree cover/deep water tables impede use, data are “available on request,” and 2018 drought is excluded from composites.
5. [Burdun et al., *Environmental Research Letters*, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/ade901): Satellite albedo/temperature shifts toward intact peatlands; **delta:** independent well depth and refusal when imaging is inconclusive. I reviewed its accessible abstract/introduction, **not** complete methods or per-site data.

A targeted 2024–26 CVPR/ICLR/NeurIPS/remote-sensing scan yielded no decisive replacement for these direct restoration incumbents; this limited search is **not** a universal novelty claim.

## Data, rights, and feasibility gate

The [Dryad package](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.qjq2bvqt2) is **1.19 MB**, with dipwell date/depth, plot-treatment and species-cover tables from 2–8 ha experimental plots. Dataset metadata specifies **CC0-1.0**, distinct from article rights. Its README has DK/FN plot codes, BOG/FOR and REE/RED/REF/RSS, but **verified per-plot coordinates/exact intervention dates were not found in the public text reviewed**. The thesis map and 2014–19 range do not solve that join. NatureScot's separate centroids (since 2013), vegetation, wells and rain are under **OGL v3.0** with attribution; centroids are not treatment polygons and raw well/rain files are request-gated. Do not assume the two programmes join.

Covariates: Landsat optical history, [Sentinel-2 L2A](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/sentinel-data/sentinel-2) 10–20 m optical since 2015 (free public access; respect Copernicus attribution), Sentinel-1 SAR after availability, [ERA5-Land](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-land) 0.1° hourly weather context **not** local well truth. Verify upstream use/redistribution terms before caching derivatives; a paper's CC BY or mirror's licence cannot license measurements. No pretrained weights are needed or presumed: climate controls, linear models and small RFs suffice. Isoaho's RF weights/Finnish field data were not verified released. Without plot locations/dates and independently reusable NatureScot wells, cross-region testing and a public Space are blocked. Training overlap is not established because no checkpoint is selected.

## Falsifier, capped at 156 scored plot–date–sensor records

**Gate zero:** without verified plot coordinates, dates and reusable cross-region wells, stop; do not manufacture labels from vegetation or pixels. If resolved, freeze site IDs, dates and hashes **before** fitting. Train/tune on six complete Hughes groups through 2021 and non-test NatureScot sites through 2021. Hold out three *entire* Hughes groups, six geographically separate NatureScot sites and **2023 everywhere**; disclose residual spatial autocorrelation between near Hughes groups. Score **3 groups × 6 roles (four treatments, FOR, BOG) × [4 optical dates (2013/14/17/21) + 2 SAR dates (2017/21)] = 108**, plus **6 external sites × 4 dates (2017/19/21/23) × 2 sensor strata = 48**: **≤156 total**. These are upper bounds, not confirmed matches: require contemporaneous wells and valid scenes, count repeat site/date/sensor records separately, exclude pre-2015 SAR and report missingness. If 2023 wells are unavailable, declare temporal transfer **untested**, not passed. Without external drained controls, omit effect-like contrasts. Resample **whole sites/groups**, not pixels.

Freeze comparisons with month-matched well climatology, rainfall-only regression, drained-reference difference-in-differences with pre-trend check, red/NIR/SWIR change, optical-only RF and optical+SAR RF; “visit for a measurement” is the safety baseline. Predeclare held-out-site mean absolute well-depth error (cm), 90% interval coverage/width, selective risk versus abstention, and group-level recovery-direction errors; site-block bootstrap intervals will be wide. Test pre-intervention placebo dates, bog negative controls, sensor/cloud outages, 2018 drought, canopy/deep-water strata and nonparallel *pre*-trends. Kill remote inference if its held-out-site MAE improvement over weather-only and simple optical baselines fails a grouped-bootstrap 95% interval test, nominal 90% intervals cover <80%, or recovery is asserted against contradictory wells/references. These are **prospective thresholds, not results**.

**Identification:** satellites predict wetness; wells describe recovery; neither alone identifies the intervention effect. Site selection, concurrent felling, drought, management and spillovers confound observational controls. Hughes's randomized treatment plots could support a separate **within-experiment enhanced-vs-standard** contrast if assignment/timing, baseline comparability and interference assumptions are established. Bog is not an untreated forest. Otherwise even parallel-trend-compatible differences remain assumption-dependent/descriptive; no carbon or biodiversity effects.

## Space: bounded, useful only after the gate

A no-login Gradio CPU Space would cache a **licensed** site/date table with source hashes and two curated examples (change; wet-year false alarm). Users select a registered site/window or enter ≤24 scalar well/rain readings in a typed form; no private coordinates, image/ZIP uploads, arbitrary files, tokens or unbounded queries. Bounded lookup/regression yields the adjusted hydrograph, drained reference, well residual/interval, missingness, pre-trend/placebo check, source/date/hash and “supported *descriptive* recovery,” “not supported” or **“abstain: visit field.”** Submitted series are **unverified**, never retained; unsupported sites are refused. Historical examples are **replay, not live inference**. Future live imagery needs new access/freshness/recalibration checks. Suppress sensitive exact well coordinates. This is a checkable field-visit decision aid, not simply a map, but adoption remains unproven.

## Subjective scores and verdict

**Potential impact 4/5:** a trustworthy false-alarm/referral check could affect costly restoration verification, but no measured savings or adoption. **Scientific openness 3/5:** CC0 experimental outcomes and OGL programme documentation exist; exact joins, request-gated records and upstream rights remain unresolved. **Feasibility 2/5:** only nine randomized blocks at one reserve, weather and canopy confounding, unknown coordinates and independent labels. **Demo usefulness 3/5:** actionable evidence/replay for registered sites if data gate clears; otherwise merely educational. **WAM fit 2/5:** prioritising the *information action* of a field visit is related, but this is not a trained world-action/dynamics model and cannot observe a physical intervention counterfactual.

**Decision:** conditional-go to verify joins/rights and run the ≤156-unit falsifier; **no-go now** for claiming a causal restoration effect, carbon/biodiversity benefits, nationwide inference, or an original satellite map. Pivot to a transparent field-versus-satellite failure benchmark if cross-region truth is unavailable; reject the domain as a Space-first scientific programme if even the source join cannot be lawfully reproduced.
