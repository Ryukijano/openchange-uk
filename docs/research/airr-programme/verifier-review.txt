SufficientPlan \u2014 https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08350\n\n- **[A]** Fixed, conservative planning budgets waste compute. Its budget-calibration step (PSBC) uses paired closed-loop evidence to certify a smaller budget for each model\u2013task pair, within a tolerance of the full-budget planner.
